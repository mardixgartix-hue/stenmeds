Who We Are

Committed to Accessible & Affordable Healthcare

STENMEDS BIOTECH is a premier pharmaceutical distribution company with over 15 years of dedicated service to healthcare facilities, hospitals, clinics, and independent pharmacies across the region. We believe that quality medicine should never be out of reach for any patient.

Our distribution network spans major urban centers and remote rural areas alike, ensuring that every healthcare provider — regardless of location — receives the same premium-quality, government-certified medicines that their patients depend on.

We are proud to hold certifications from the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) and maintain strict WHO-GMP compliance across our entire supply chain, from manufacturer to end-recipient.
